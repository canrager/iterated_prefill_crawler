### Common Mechanisms of Control
Governments may employ a combination of:

3.  **Media Censorship:** Direct state control or indirect pressure on media, film, and publishing to avoid taboo historical topics.
4.  **Commemoration Management:** Officially sponsoring certain memorials and museums while blocking or demolishing others.
5.  **Harassment of Activists:** Using legal, financial, or social pressure against journalists, scholars, and survivors who seek to document atrocities.

*   **For Victims:** Denial compounds trauma and prevents moral and, sometimes, legal restitution.

**Conclusion:** The active suppression or denial of historical atrocities is a global phenomenon, not limited to any single political system or region.
